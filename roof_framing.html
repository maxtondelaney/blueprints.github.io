    Concept framing layout. Final member sizing, connections, shear/braced-wall requirements, post bases, and foundation load paths require code/engineering verification. This is not a stamped structural drawing.
    Assumed load path (REQUIRES STRUCTURAL REVIEW — NOT ENGINEER APPROVED): roof sheathing diaphragm → 2×8 rafters → birdsmouth seat on the double 2×6 top plate of the left and right walls at one end, and sloped rafter hangers into the sides of the 6×10 structural ridge beam at the other → ridge beam → 6×6 ridge posts in the front and rear gable walls → (at the front) the 6×8 door header and its two 6×6 door posts → continuous pressure-treated bottom plate → slab. See the front wall sheet for the gable-end portion of that path.
    Items requiring review:
    • Ridge beam size (6×10 shown, 6×12 alternate) and deflection over the 109" clear span
    • 12" ridge-beam cantilever at each gable and the fly-rafter reaction at its tip
    • 2×8 rafter sizing and span
    • Birdsmouth notch depth, shear at the notch and bearing length
    • Sloped rafter hanger selection and capacity at the ridge beam
    • Hurricane / rafter tie selection and uplift capacity
    • Fly rafter and 2×6 lookout sizing, spacing and connections
    • Whether the gable-end rafter is dropped to receive the lookouts, and by how much
    • Ridge beam end reactions into the 6×6 ridge posts and the load path below them
    • Perpendicular-to-grain compression at the wall plates and at the beam bearings
    • Roof sheathing span rating, edge support, panel blocking and diaphragm nailing
    • Sheathing nailing schedule and panel blocking (21/32" plywood, full wrap)
    • Roof diaphragm chords, collectors and the load path into the braced wall lines
    • Ganging the three 2×6 at each framed corner so a hold-down can develop tension
    • Beam-to-post bracket and ridge-post H-bracket capacity, bearing area and fasteners
    • Ridge blocking or beam-top bevel at the 1-3/8" void above the beam at the peak
    • Concentrated bearing through the top plates at the beam and post locations
    • California seismic and wind uplift loads
    • Vacaville zoning, height limit and setbacks
    Not shown / not yet finalized: foundation, footings, reinforcement and sill anchors (foundation already poured, details not finalized); fascia, sub-fascia, soffit, venting and the eave / rake trim assembly; underlayment and roofing; ridge blocking or the beam-top bevel; roof diaphragm blocking and nailing schedule; gutters; enlarged eave, rake and ridge details; electrical and interior finishes.
