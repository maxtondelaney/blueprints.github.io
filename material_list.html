What this is. An order-ready framing take-off read directly off the five framing sheets in this set — front_wall_framing.html, left_wall_framing.html, right_wall_framing.html, rear_wall_framing.html and roof_framing.html. Every count and length below is recomputed here from the same dimension model those sheets use, so it moves if the design moves. Lumber is converted into common stock lengths and paired with a cutting plan. The plan includes a 1/8" saw kerf per cut but no spare boards for damage, defects or field changes. Fasteners, adhesives, hangers' nails and blocking schedules remain excluded because the drawings do not specify them. See Purchase-plan assumptions, Basis of quantities and Not included below.

Validation

    Concept framing layout. Final member sizing, connections, shear/braced-wall requirements, base-of-wall detailing, post bases, hold-downs and foundation load paths require code and engineering verification. This take-off counts what the drawings show; it does not certify that what the drawings show is adequate. Quantities requiring review before ordering:
    • Ridge beam size — 6×10 shown, 6×12 flagged as the alternate on the roof sheet
    • 6×8 door header under the concentrated ridge reaction
    • Window header sizing at all four openings
    • Rafter size and spacing (2×8 @ 16" o.c. maximum shown)
    • Through-lookout, gable-line blocking and roof-edge connection capacities
    • Whether one hold-down per building corner serves the piers in both wall directions
    • Hold-down, post-base, bracket, hanger and tie models — none selected anywhere in the set
    • Sheathing span rating, edge nailing, panel blocking and the bracing schedule
    • Plate splice locations and lap at the corners
    • Treated-lumber contact: corrosion resistance of every connector touching the P.T. sill plate
    • Whether the narrow shear piers can be qualified as drawn, which may add portal-frame hardware
